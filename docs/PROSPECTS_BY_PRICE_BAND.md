**Important caveats**

- These are **publicly named** YugabyteDB adopters (case studies, blogs, DSS talks) — not a private customer list.
- Band assignment is **inferred fit for your Ops/FinOps platform** (estate complexity, compliance, multi-cloud), **not** their Yugabyte license spend.
- Confirm live usage via LinkedIn, job posts, and DSS talks before investing a full sales cycle.
- **Do not** email personal contacts scraped from the web; use company careers/partner/alliances and LinkedIn titles (VP Platform, DBA, Data Platform).

## Price bands (reminder)

| Band | Deal range | Typical estate |
|------|------------|----------------|
| **Small** | ~$150K – $350K | ~5–20 universes / simpler footprint |
| **Mid** | ~$350K – $900K | ~20–80 universes, multi-env, security + FinOps |
| **Large** | ~$900K – $2M+ | 100+ universes or Fortune-scale / regulated |

## Who to contact (titles)

- VP / Director **Platform Engineering**, **Database Strategy**, **Infrastructure**, **SRE**
- Head of **Data Platform** / **DBA** / **Cloud Operations**
- For mid/large: also **CISO org** (vulns/certs) and **FinOps** (chargeback)

LinkedIn search pattern: `"YugabyteDB" AND "<Company>"` or `"Yugabyte" AND "universe"`.

## Partner-amplified prospects

Ask Yugabyte alliances for intros into:

- Customers running **YugabyteDB Anywhere (YBA)** with many universes

- AWS/GCP joint accounts modernizing off Oracle/Cassandra onto YB  

Your pitch: complementary **ops + FinOps + security** layer — not competing with YBA.
